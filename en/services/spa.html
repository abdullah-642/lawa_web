Spa Projects

Luxury Spa Design & Build in Riyadh and Saudi Arabia

From steam rooms and Moroccan baths to salt caves and saunas — we transform a space into a complete relaxation experience that blends luxury with high operational performance, through professional insulation and precise execution.

At Liwa Concept, we deliver integrated solutions for the design and build of luxury spa facilities to the highest technical and operational standards — from insulation and moisture treatment through to steam and heating systems and final finishes. Here are our precise specializations within spa projects.

01
Steam Rooms & Moroccan Baths

Design & Build of Steam Rooms and Moroccan Baths

We deliver integrated solutions for the design and build of steam rooms and Moroccan baths to the highest technical and operational standards, from insulation work and moisture treatment through to the installation of steam and heating systems and the final finishes.

We rely on specialized insulation systems that resist steam and humidity, along with vaulted ceilings, water drainage systems, and carefully engineered ventilation to ensure operational efficiency and a long service life for the project.

  • Design and build of luxury Moroccan baths
  • Build of commercial and hotel steam rooms
  • Smart steam systems with digital control
  • Underfloor heating and heated treatment beds
  • Marble, natural stone, and mosaic work
  • Lighting systems and starlit fiber-optic ceilings
  • Ventilation and humidity-control systems

We transform spaces into a complete relaxation experience that blends luxury with high operational performance.

02
Salt Rooms

Design & Build of Therapeutic Salt Caves

We build professional salt rooms and caves designed to provide a complete relaxation environment that combines natural design with modern technology.

Salt caves feature walls clad in natural Himalayan salt with precise systems for dispersing dry salt particles throughout the room, creating a unique sensory experience that helps enhance relaxation and improve the air quality within the space.

  • Design and build of commercial salt rooms and wellness resorts
  • Natural Himalayan salt wall and ceiling cladding
  • Salt-particle generation and dispersion systems (Halogenerator)
  • Humidity and temperature control systems
  • Custom salt lighting and unique aesthetic designs
  • Ventilation systems engineered specifically for salt rooms

We design each room to suit the project's identity and the intended customer experience.

03
Saunas

Design & Build of Saunas

We provide integrated solutions for building dry and wet saunas for wellness centers, resorts, hotels, and sports clubs.

Saunas are manufactured using high-quality woods that resist heat and humidity, with advanced heating systems that ensure even heat distribution and a comfortable, safe user experience.

  • Traditional Finnish sauna
  • Modern electric sauna
  • Commercial and hotel sauna
  • Smart control systems for temperature and humidity
  • Concealed lighting and therapeutic lighting
  • Design and build to suit specific spaces and requirements

We focus on combining operational quality with refined design to deliver a complete relaxation experience.

04
Custom Spa Furniture

Bespoke Spa Furniture Manufacturing

We bring specialized expertise to the design and manufacture of professional spa furniture that aligns with the project's identity and the demands of daily operation.

We manufacture massage beds, treatment units, and custom furniture using high-quality materials, with a wide range of options for colors, finishes, and technical details.

  • Custom-built massage beds
  • Heating systems integrated within the beds
  • Selection of colors and fabrics according to the visual identity
  • Storage units and dedicated spa operational furniture
  • Furniture for treatment and skincare rooms
  • Furniture for Moroccan baths and steam rooms
  • Execution of bespoke designs to order

We craft every piece to combine comfort, durability, and a luxurious appearance.

05
Heating Systems for Moroccan Baths

Specialized Heating Systems for Moroccan Baths

Heating is one of the most important elements behind a successful Moroccan bath, which is why we provide professional heating systems designed specifically to deliver thermal comfort and maintain operational efficiency.

  • Heating for Moroccan bath beds
  • Hydronic and electric underfloor heating
  • Heating for seating areas and marble benches
  • Smart temperature control systems
  • Energy-efficient solutions for commercial projects

Each system is designed according to the nature of the project and the scale of use to ensure even heat distribution and the highest levels of comfort for guests.
