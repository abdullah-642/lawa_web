Villas & Residential Spaces

Designing & building luxury villas and palaces in Riyadh and Saudi Arabia

From villas and palaces to majlis and premium kitchens — we deliver your residential spaces with refined touches that reflect your personality and discerning taste, at the highest levels of luxury and craftsmanship.

At Liwa Concept we design and build homes that reflect their owner's personality and discerning taste down to the finest detail, with no compromise on quality — we accompany your project on a complete journey from the first idea through to turnkey handover. Villas

We offer complete solutions for designing and building luxury villas in a refined style that reflects the owner's personality, from the layout of spaces and their functions through to the selection of materials and final finishes.

  • Luxury materials and finishes

  • Architectural lighting
  • Full execution supervision
