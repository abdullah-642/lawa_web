Restaurants

Restaurant Design & Build

We deliver complete solutions for designing and building luxury restaurants that combine design beauty with daily operational efficiency — from the layout of dining halls and seating areas through to facades and final finishes.

In every detail we account for the service flow and guest comfort, with considered acoustic treatments, lighting and sound systems, and finishing materials that withstand heavy use and keep their luster over time.

  • Design of dining halls and seating areas
  • Operational layout that serves service efficiency
  • Lighting and sound systems
  • Acoustic treatments
  • Finishing materials resistant to heavy use
  • Execution of facades and interior decor

We design the restaurant to be an unforgettable experience that brings the guest back.

  • Contemporary cafe concepts

  • Integration of the brand's visual identity

Shops & Showrooms

Retail Shop & Showroom Design & Build

We build retail shops, showrooms and display halls with a design that highlights the product and guides the customer's eye toward the purchase decision, through considered display and lighting systems.

We engineer customer movement paths within the space, and design attractive display fronts and flexible shelving systems that give every product the standing it deserves.

  • Retail shop design
  • Display halls and showrooms
  • Display and shelving systems
  • Product-highlighting lighting
  • Organization of customer movement paths
  • Attractive display fronts

We design the space to sell — with a shopping experience that drives the purchase.

04
Commercial Kitchens

Commercial Kitchen Planning & Build

The kitchen is the beating heart of the restaurant, so we plan and build commercial kitchens with a professionalism that ensures workflow, production efficiency and operational safety.

We install extraction and ventilation systems, hygienic and resistant floors and walls, and distribute equipment to achieve the highest efficiency — in full compliance with municipality and Civil Defense requirements.

  • Professional commercial kitchen planning
  • Extraction and ventilation systems
  • Hygienic, resistant floors and walls
  • Equipment layout for work efficiency
  • Compliance with municipality and Civil Defense requirements
  • Storage and refrigeration solutions

A kitchen that runs efficiently is the foundation of a restaurant's success.

  • Exterior facade design
  • Signage and wayfinding
  • Integration of the visual identity into the space
  • Glass facades and architectural lighting
  • Visual attraction elements

The storefront is the first impression — we design it to attract and stay in the memory.
