Offices & Corporate HQs

Design & build of offices & corporate HQs in Riyadh & Saudi Arabia

From executive offices and corporate headquarters to meeting rooms and flexible workspaces — we design work environments that combine functional efficiency with visual prestige and reflect your organisation's standing.

At Liwa Concept we design workspaces that boost productivity and reflect a company's identity and standing — from the first concept through to final, turnkey handover. We blend a precise functional study of workflow with luxurious finishes to create a work environment worthy of your organisation's name. Here are our specific specialisations within office and corporate HQ projects.

Executive Offices

  • Senior management office design
  • Luxurious materials and refined finishes
  • Bespoke office furniture

  • Acoustic insulation for privacy
